Nothing I like comes in one material. A good day is a song, a conversation, a bad coffee, a photo I took by accident, and a sentence from a book that won't leave me alone. It doesn't file neatly. So I stopped trying to make this site file neatly too.

## Collage as a way of thinking

A collage doesn't pretend the pieces belong together. It puts a ticket stub next to a leaf next to a stamp and lets you figure out why. I like that it's <Mark>honest about being assembled</Mark>. You can see the tape.

That's also why every page here looks different. The notes are quiet and dark, the resources are loud and Swiss, the homepage is paper and scissors. <Scribble>People are inconsistent</Scribble>, and I'd rather the site admit it.

<PullQuote>Humans are inherently incoherent. That's part of the beauty of it.</PullQuote>

## What goes here

Mostly things I want to remember. Some finished thoughts, some half-baked ones. If a note has pictures, they'll be here. If it needs a song, I'll find a way.
